## 4. Cash Flow & Capital Allocation (Q1 FY25 → Q2 FY26)

- **Operating cash flow:** $27.9B–$53.9B per quarter, peaking in Q4 FY25; **FCF $24.4B–$51.6B** per quarter → TTM FCF **$107.7B** (~23% FCF margin on TTM revenue).
- **Shareholder returns:** Buybacks of **$12.3B–$25.1B/quarter** plus dividends of **~$3.8B–$4.0B/quarter** — roughly $16B–$29B returned per quarter, all funded internally.
- **Debt management:** Conservative; repayments peaked at $8.1B in Q4 FY25 (visible in the balance-sheet deleveraging above).
- **CapEx:** Modest $2.0B–$3.5B/quarter — low reinvestment intensity preserves FCF.
- **Period-end cash:** $35.9B–$45.6B across the window — ample liquidity despite the payout pace.

This is a capital-return machine: FCF yield on a $4.91T market cap ≈ **2.2%**, and the buyback program is shrinking the share base (~12.6B shares implied), providing structural EPS support independent of growth.

## 5. Valuation Assessment

- **TTM P/E 39.05 / Forward P/E 35.11** — a ~10% embedded discount to TTM implies the market is pricing ~10%+ EPS growth ahead, which the +16.4% Q2 revenue print and margin expansion make plausible.
- **PEG 2.67** — on a growth-adjusted basis, AAPL trades at a premium; cheap-money "quality" multiples are stretched.
- **P/B 45.74** — optically extreme, but meaningless as a primary screen here; Apple's buyback program deliberately minimizes book equity.
- **Verdict on valuation:** Fully (arguably richly) valued, but earnings quality, deleveraging, and share count shrinkage justify a quality premium. The risk is multiple compression, not fundamental deterioration.

## 6. Risks

1. **Multiple compression risk** — at ~39x trailing, any growth miss (iPhone cycle, services deceleration) hits the stock harder than fundamentals.
2. **R&D spend ramp** — R&D up ~32% to $11.7B/quarter; if AI/next-gen initiatives don't convert to product demand, margin pressure follows.
3. **Seasonality** — Q4 (holiday) accounts for ~31% of TTM revenue; quarterly prints will swing.
