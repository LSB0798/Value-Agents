

For **AAPL**, I agree with the bear-leaning tactical call: the business quality is real, but the trade is not attractive from current levels.

**Key analysis:**
- **Fundamentals remain strong:** revenue growth, margin profile, free cash flow, and balance-sheet strength support Apple’s core quality story.
- **But the entry is poor:** **AAPL** is near the high-$330s/low-$340s area, extended, and expensive at roughly 39x TTM earnings and 35x forward earnings.
- **Risk/reward is unfavorable:** upside toward **$355–$360** is only a few percent, while a loss of support near **$322** opens a materially larger drawdown.
- **Momentum is fading:** the stock is not showing a clean continuation setup, and volatility is elevated ahead of a binary earnings event.
- **Earnings risk is high:** **AAPL** needs near-flawless execution, revenue confirmation, margin strength, and guidance support. That is too much to assume before paying a rich multiple.

**Decision:**  
Reduce or exit tactical **AAPL** exposure into strength, especially near **$338–$341**. If flat, do not chase. A better re-entry setup exists on a pullback to **$322–$329** with stabilized momentum, or after a post-earnings confirmation and retest.
