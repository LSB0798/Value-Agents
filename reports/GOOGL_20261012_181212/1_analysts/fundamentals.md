

# GOOGL (Alphabet) — Comprehensive Fundamental Analysis Report
**Report Date:** 2026-10-09 | **Ticker:** GOOGL | **Sector:** Technology / Internet

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Alphabet (GOOGL) is a **$4.3 trillion mega-cap** with exceptional scale, profitability, and liquidity. TTM revenue of **$445.87B** and TTM net income of **$244.12B** underscore dominant market position. However, the data reveals a critical **earnings-quality caveat**: headline results are inflated by **$98.8B in one-time gains on security sales**, and the company is undergoing an **aggressive AI-infrastructure pivot** that has flipped free cash flow negative and pushed net debt sharply higher. This is fundamentally a *strong company in a heavy capital-expenditure phase*, not a story of operational distress.

**Overall Sentiment: Positive (with caution)** — strong profitability and balance sheet offset by one-time earnings distortion and rising leverage from AI capex.

## 3. Income Statement — Q2 2026 (Most Recent)
- **Revenue: $119.8B** (vs. $96.4B in Q2 2025) → **+24.3% YoY**, robust top-line expansion.
- **Gross Profit: $73.9B** → **~61.7% gross margin**, indicating high-margin, software-led economics.
- **Operating Income: $40.8B** → **~34.0% operating margin**.
- **Operating Expenses: $33.1B** — R&D **$18.2B** + SG&A **$14.9B**. Heavy R&D spend reflects AI investment.
- **Net Income: $112.2B** — **WARNING: driven by $98.8B in gains on security sales. Normalized (ex-items) net income was only ~$32.2B.**
- **Diluted EPS: $9.11** (vs. $2.31 prior year) — again, inflated by the one-time gain.

**Insight:** Core operating performance is excellent (34% op margin, 24% revenue growth). But **~88% of reported Q2 net income is non-operating/one-time**. A trader modeling "earnings growth" off the $9.11 EPS would materially overestimate the sustainable run-rate.

## 4. Balance Sheet — Q2 2026 (as of June 30, 2026)
- **Total Assets: $922.0B** (vs. $502.1B in Q2 2025) → **~84% increase YoY**, reflecting massive asset buildout.
- **Total Equity: $640.5B** — retained earnings $493.4B, capital stock $149.4B (incl. $18.0B preferred).
- **Total Liabilities: $281.5B** — current $126.1B, non-current $155.4B.
- **Total Debt: $112.8B** → **Net Debt: $42.3B** (vs. just $2.6B in June 2025). **A ~$40B YoY jump in net debt.**
  - Long-term debt $98.2B; capital lease obligations $14.6B.
- **Cash + Equivalents + ST Investments: $242.5B** — fortress liquidity.
- **Net PPE: $338.9B** — huge fixed-asset base (AI data centers/servers).
- **Available-for-Sale Securities: $131.5B**; Goodwill & Intangibles: $66.9B.

**Insight:** The balance sheet grew nearly **$420B YoY**, almost entirely PPE and cash/investments. The **net debt surge ($2.6B → $42.3B)** signals Alphabet is now *borrowing to fund the AI capex cycle*. With $242.5B in liquidity, solvency risk is negligible (current ratio 2.72), but **leverage is a structural change to monitor.**

## 5. Cash Flow — Q2 2026
- **Operating Cash Flow: $39.1B** — down from $45.8B (Q1 2026) and $52.4B (Q4 2025); three-quarter downtrend.
- **Capital Expenditures: $44.9B** — exceeds operating cash flow.
- **Free Cash Flow: −$5.9B** — a **sharp reversal** from +$10.1B (Q1 2026) and +$24.6B (Q4 2025).
- **Investing Cash Flow: −$82.4B** — $66.6B investment purchases + $44.9B PPE, partially offset by $29.5B investment sales.
- **Financing Cash Flow: +$61.2B** — funded by **$24.8B debt issuance** and **$30.5B net common stock issuance** (dilution), less $3.8B debt repayment.
- **Cash Balance: $55.9B** (up from $38.1B in Q1 2026).
- Net income from continuing operations: $112.2B (vs. $62.6B in Q1 2026).

**Insight:** The **negative FCF is a strategic pivot, not operational distress** — the company is *choosing* to spend on AI infrastructure faster than it generates cash, and is **funding the gap with both debt ($24.8B) and new equity ($30.5B)**. The **$30.5B equity issuance = shareholder dilution** is a key downside factor for per-share holders. Traders should track whether FCF inflects back to positive as the capex cycle matures.

## 7. Key Risks & Opportunities
**Risks (Downside)**
1. **Earnings quality:** ~88% of Q2 net income is one-time; normalized EPS ~2.6x lower than reported.
2. **Negative FCF:** Capex outpacing operating cash flow; FCF could stay negative through the AI buildout.
3. **Rising leverage:** Net debt up ~$40B YoY to $42.3B; debt-funded capex.
4. **Dilution:** $30.5B net equity issuance dilutes per-share economics.
5. **Valuation re-rating risk:** Forward P/E of 23.1x is a premium multiple; if AI capex ROI disappoints, multiple could compress.

**Opportunities (Upside)**
1. **24% YoY revenue growth** with 34% operating margin — rare scale + growth + profitability combination.
2. **Fortress liquidity** ($242.5B cash/investments, 2.72 current ratio) — negligible solvency risk.
3. **AI investment cycle** — massive PPE/R&D buildout positions the company to monetize AI demand.
4. **Cheap-looking TTM P/E (17.6x)** could attract value-oriented flows if one-time gains roll off gradually rather than abruptly.

---

## 8. Actionable Takeaways for Traders
1. **Normalize earnings before modeling.** Underwrite ~$15 forward EPS, not $19.93 TTM. The "cheap" 17.6x TTM P/E is an illusion from one-time gains; **23.1x forward is the real multiple.**
2. **Do not panic on negative FCF.** It reflects deliberate AI capex, not cash-flow failure. But *do* monitor the OCF 3-quarter downtrend — if it accelerates while capex stays flat, that's a red flag.
3. **Track the funding mix.** Each quarter, check whether capex is funded by OCF (healthy) or by debt/equity issuance (dilution + leverage). Continued $25B+ equity issuances cap per-share upside.
4. **Position for volatility.** Trading between 50-day/200-day averages with a $239–$409 range → use wider stops and consider options/position-sizing over pure directional bets.
5. **Catalyst watch:** AI revenue/monetization commentary and the next FCF inflection are the two variables that will most likely move the stock and its multiple.
