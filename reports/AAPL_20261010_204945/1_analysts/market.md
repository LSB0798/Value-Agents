

# AAPL Technical Analysis Report — 2026-10-09

## 1. Market Context & Indicator Selection Rationale

AAPL has posted a **+33% six-month rally** ($255.17 → $340.42), with a sharp June correction ($273.51 low), a sustained recovery, and a **cycle peak of $345.34 on September 22**. For a market in this state — a mature uptrend currently consolidating near highs — the key questions are: (1) Is the strategic trend intact? (2) Is short-term momentum decaying or resetting? (3) Where are the precise support/resistance levels? (4) How should risk be sized given changing volatility?

I therefore selected 8 complementary indicators spanning four non-overlapping categories:
- **Trend (3 timeframes):** `close_200_sma`, `close_50_sma`, `close_10_ema` — no two measure the same horizon
- **Momentum (2):** `macd` (state) + `macdh` (acceleration/divergence) — complementary, not redundant
- **Overbought/Oversold (1):** `rsi`
- **Volatility/Structure (2):** `boll` (dynamic benchmark) + `atr` (risk sizing)

## 2. Trend Structure — Firmly Bullish, All Timeframes Aligned

- **200 SMA ($290.40, rising from $279.12 since Aug 10):** The strategic benchmark is in a steady, uninterrupted ascent. Price at $340.42 sits **~17% above** it. The long-term trend is unambiguously up, and the 200 SMA provides a far-below, ultimate dynamic support zone (~$290).
- **50 SMA ($322.36, rising from $309.44):** The medium-term slope is steeply upward, confirming institutional accumulation over the past quarter. This is the key **intermediate support**: a pullback to ~$322 would be a trend-healthy entry zone.
- **10 EMA ($335.52, rising from $315.03):** Short-term averages remain elevated and rising. Price ($340.42) sits just ~1.5% above it — the stock is consolidating *on top of* its short-term trend, not breaking away from it.

**Alignment verdict:** Price > 10 EMA > 50 SMA > 200 SMA, with all three averages rising — a textbook bullish alignment. No timeframe is in conflict.

## 3. Momentum — The Critical Nuance: Cooling but Stabilizing

- **MACD line:** Zero-line crossover on **August 28** confirmed the resumption of bullish momentum. It peaked at **6.272 on September 22** (coincident with the price high of $345.34) and has since retreated to **3.437**. Still decisively positive — the trend impulse is not dead — but the rate of ascent has roughly halved.
- **MACD Histogram:** This is the most important warning signal in the current setup. It peaked at **+1.68 on September 17**, crossed negative on **September 28**, accelerated to **−1.07 on October 5**, and has since **stabilized at −0.45 on October 9**. Interpretation: the post-September-22 pullback was an orderly **momentum reset, not a trend breakdown** — the histogram is contracting back toward zero, which historically precedes either a bullish re-cross (resumption) or further drift. Watch for a positive re-cross as the trigger for renewed upside.
- **Notably, there is no bearish divergence failure here:** MACD's peak (Sept 22) coincided exactly with the price high, so the momentum read-through is *coincident cooling*, not deceptive strength.

## 4. RSI — Clean Washout of Overbought Conditions

RSI swung from **40.36 (Aug 12)** to **66.92 (Sept 22)** — it never even reached the 70 overbought threshold during the final push, which is a sign of orderly, accumulation-driven strength rather than froth. It has since normalized to **56.14**, a balanced, slightly bullish mid-50s reading. This is the *healthiest* possible position for an uptrend continuation: enough downside air before any oversold signal, and no overbought penalty on the table.

## 5. Volatility & Price Structure — Compression Precedes Expansion

- **Bollinger Middle (20 SMA) at $335.39** — price at $340.42 is trading in the **upper half of the bands**, the bullish zone. Crucially, the **Bollinger middle ($335.39) and 10 EMA ($335.52) form a confluence support shelf at ~$335.40–335.50**. Holding this zone is the single most important short-term tell.
- **ATR has compressed from 8.91 (early August) to 6.75**, a ~24% decline. Daily range is now ~**2.0% of price**. Volatility contraction after a +33% rally typically resolves in a **directional expansion** — the setup is coiled, not broken.

## 6. Actionable Insights

1. **Primary support shelf: $335.40–335.50** (10 EMA + Bollinger middle confluence). A close below this invalidates the short-term structure and opens the door to the 50 SMA at **$322.36** — a high-conviction add zone given the rising long-term trend.
2. **Resistance: $345.34** (September 22 high). A daily close above it with a positive MACD-histogram re-cross would confirm trend resumption; the measured target on breakout is a re-test of the June–April structure highs and new territory.
3. **Risk parameters from ATR ($6.75):** For a new entry at ~$340, place stops **1.5× ATR ≈ $10 below** (≈$330, just under the EMA/Bollinger shelf and above the 50 SMA). Position size accordingly — a $340 name with a $10 stop represents ~3% risk per share, or scale positions so the stop represents ≤1% of portfolio capital per trade.
4. **Timing:** Do **not** chase at $340.42 with a negative MACD histogram. Either (a) wait for the histogram to re-cross positive above $345.34, or (b) buy the retest of $335.40–335.50 with a tight stop under $330.
5. **Invalidation of the bullish thesis:** A weekly close below the 50 SMA ($322) would shift the medium-term picture from "consolidation in an uptrend" to "potential deeper correction toward the 200 SMA (~$290)." Until then, pullbacks remain buyable.
6. **Income note:** $0.27 dividends paid May 11 and August 10 (~0.8% trailing yield) provide modest carry while waiting for the breakout.

**Net assessment:** The strategic and medium-term trend are firmly bullish and aligned across all three moving averages. The short-term picture shows an orderly post-peak momentum reset (negative but stabilizing MACD histogram, RSI normalized to 56, price hugging its 10 EMA) inside a volatility compression — a classic coil ahead of directional resolution. This is a **hold/accumulate-on-dips** posture, not a sell, and not an aggressive buy at current prices.
