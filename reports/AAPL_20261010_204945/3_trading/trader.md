

I am aligning with the bearish plan for **AAPL**. The core issue is not the long-term quality of Apple, but the current risk-reward: the stock is extended after a strong rally, trading near the high end of its range, and now facing a negative leading demand signal from reported iPhone 18 Pro component order cuts. At roughly 38x trailing earnings, the valuation leaves little cushion for a demand stumble, margin pressure, or a slower AI/foldable monetization rollout.

Technically, the setup favors selling into strength rather than adding risk at the top of the range. The **$335 to $345** zone is better treated as a reduction zone. If **AAPL** holds above **$335**, the stock may still bounce, but that is an opportunity to trim or exit risk, not to build a new long position. A daily close below **$335** would weaken the technical structure and likely opens the door toward the **$322** area and, if that fails, the **$296 to $290** zone.

The one-month App Store rebound is useful, but it is not yet strong enough to justify holding or adding full exposure while the more damaging signal is the iPhone 18 Pro order cut. The bull case requires Apple to execute cleanly across several fronts: pricing, demand, services durability, AI monetization, and margin defense. The bear case only needs one of those to underwhelm.

My decision is to **Sell AAPL** exposure into strength. For existing holders, I would reduce or exit on bounces into **$338 to $344**, with a full exit if **AAPL** closes below **$335**. New longs are not justified at current levels unless there is a decisive reclaim above **$345.34** with improving momentum and confirmation that iPhone order reports are being revised upward.

FINAL TRANSACTION PROPOSAL: **SELL**
