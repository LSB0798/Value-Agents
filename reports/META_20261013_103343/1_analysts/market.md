**Momentum/Reversal (RSI)** — selected for overbought/oversold state and divergence after a 49% run.

An RSI in the low-to-mid 50s in an uptrend is a textbook buying zone, not a sell signal — provided it doesn't lose 50 (which would signal momentum failure).

**Support floor (Bollinger Lower Band)** — selected (in place of upper/middle bands to avoid redundancy with the SMAs) to quantify where the dynamic downside floor sits after the bands re-anchored.
