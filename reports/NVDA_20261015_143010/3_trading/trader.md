

## NVDA Trading Recommendation

**Decision: BUY NVDA — but only as a staged, confirmation-based, risk-managed trade.**

The strongest case for NVDA is not just the AI narrative; it is the confirmed financial quality: exceptional cash flow generation, extreme margins, high revenue and earnings growth, net-cash strength, and a forward multiple that still appears low relative to that growth. A 13.9x forward earnings multiple and a 0.28 PEG do not eliminate risk, but they reduce the likelihood that NVDA is being priced like a pure speculative bubble. The bull case is stronger on confirmed evidence.

However, the bear case is valid on execution risk: NVDA has almost no cushion for a meaningful capex pause, competitor wins from AMD, Broadcom, or custom silicon are real, and the technical structure has not fully reclaimed the 50-day moving average or the $201 VWMA. The rebound has not been fully proven, and adding too aggressively into the $201–$209 resistance zone would repeat the mistake of treating support or strength as confirmation.

Therefore, the correct approach is not to sell, but to buy NVDA selectively and with discipline.

---

## Recommended Execution Plan for NVDA

### 1. Initial Entry
**Buy 1/3 of the planned NVDA position** in the **$190–$195** zone.

This entry respects the correction while participating in the cash-flow-driven bull case. It avoids chasing NVDA above the $201 VWMA before confirmation.

---

### 2. Hard Stop
**Exit the initial NVDA position if NVDA prints a daily close below $188.**

This is the thesis-invalidating level. A daily close below $188 suggests the correction is resuming and that the market no longer accepts the AI infrastructure demand story at these levels.

The risk from the $190–$195 entry to the $188 stop is roughly **3.5%**, and total portfolio risk should be capped at approximately **1% of total equity** to account for gap risk from capex news, competitive news, or memory-cycle headlines.

---

### 3. Second Entry
**Add a second 1/3 only if NVDA holds the $190 zone and forms a higher low.**

This confirms that buyers are defending the correction and that the technical base is strengthening.

---

### 4. Third Entry
**Add the final 1/3 only after NVDA prints a daily close above $201.**

Stronger conviction would be warranted if NVDA pushes toward or through **$209**, because that would confirm that the unproven rebound is turning into a continuation move.

---

## Targets for NVDA

After the position is fully established:

If NVDA reclaims $201 and then $209, trail the stop up toward breakeven and then protect gains as momentum continues.

## If You Already Own NVDA

If you already hold NVDA, the recommended action is still **BUY**, but only through the staged confirmation framework above. Do not aggressively add into the $201–$209 zone without a confirmed daily close. That area is where the bear case says the rebound is unproven. If NVDA is already at or above resistance, it is a better place to trim into strength or buy protection than to pile in blindly.

---

## Why This Is a Buy, Not a Hold

Selling NVDA would mean giving up a business with elite cash conversion, high margins, and multi-year AI infrastructure demand. Holding without a plan would leave the trade exposed to technical failure or premature strength. The disciplined path is to **buy NVDA in tranches**, use a hard stop, and only increase risk after confirmation.

The core lesson is clear: do not treat support as confirmation. Use the $188 stop, the $190 higher-low requirement, and the $201 breakout as objective triggers.

FINAL TRANSACTION PROPOSAL: **BUY**
