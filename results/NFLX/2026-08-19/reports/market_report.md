

# NFLX Technical Analysis Report — 2026-08-19

## 1. Market Context & Indicator Selection Rationale

NFLX is in a complex transitional state: a **12-month primary downtrend** (from the $124.51 peak on 2025-08-18 to the $68.95 low on 2026-07-17, a ~45% drawdown) overlaid with a **vigorous 6-week recovery** (low of $68.95 → $77.77, +12.8%). The core trading question is therefore: *Is this a trend reversal or a bear-market rally?* To answer that, I selected 8 complementary indicators spanning all major analytical dimensions:

| Dimension | Indicator | Why Selected |
|---|---|---|
| Long-term trend | close_200_sma | Establishes the strategic regime; defines major overhead resistance |
| Medium-term trend | close_50_sma | Dynamic support/resistance during the recovery |
| Short-term momentum | close_10_ema | Tracks the immediacy of the current bounce |
| Trend-momentum | macd | Crossover confirmation of momentum regime shift |
| Momentum strength | macdh | Early divergence detection and momentum acceleration |
| Oscillator | rsi | Oversold capitulation → recovery magnitude; divergence check |
| Risk/volatility | atr | Stop-loss calibration and position sizing in a high-volatility regime |
| Volume | vwma | Confirms whether the bounce has institutional volume backing |

*(Note: MACD signal line was deliberately omitted as redundant with MACD + histogram; RSI was chosen over other oscillators as the single momentum gauge.)*

## 2. Long-Term Trend (close_200_sma) — BEARISH, DOMINANT

- The 200-day SMA has fallen from **$102.03 (May 21) → $88.73 (Aug 19)**, a 13-point erosion of the long-term valuation baseline.
- Price at **$77.77 is sitting ~12% BELOW the 200-day SMA**, with the SMA still in free-fall.
- **Interpretation:** The strategic regime is decisively bearish. Every prior rally (Feb 2026's $96, Apr 2026's $107.79) sold into this descending average. The $88–89 zone is now the **single most important resistance level** on the chart — until price reclaims and holds above it, the 12-month downtrend remains technically intact.

## 3. Medium-Term Trend (close_50_sma) — BEARISH BUT IMPROVING

- The 50-day SMA slid from **$93.80 (May 21) → $74.59 (Aug 19)** — still declining, but the *rate* of decline is clearly decelerating.
- Critically, **price ($77.77) has now reclaimed the 50-day SMA ($74.59)** after trading below it for most of June–July.
- **Interpretation:** The 50-day SMA flips from resistance to **dynamic support** — but only if it flattens. A reclaim over a *falling* 50-SMA has historically marked bear-market rallies, not reversals. Watch whether the 50-SMA stops falling by mid-September; that would be the first structural sign of a bottom.

## 4. Short-Term Momentum (close_10_ema) — BULLISH

- The 10-EMA bottomed at **$70.56 on July 27**, has since rallied +8.66% to **$76.67**, and price is trading above it — the classic short-term bullish alignment.
- Key micro-resistance: the **$78** level identified by the EMA's recovery path; key micro-support: **$70.56**.
- **Interpretation:** Immediate momentum favors the bulls, but the 10-EMA is still 13.5% below its May 21 peak of $88.68 — the short-term trend is only *recovering*, not yet *reversing* the medium-term trend.

## 5. Momentum Regime (macd + macdh) — BULLISH TURN CONFIRMED

This is the **strongest bullish evidence set** in the analysis:

- **MACD line** crossed from negative to **positive between Aug 11–12** (−0.07 → −0.02 → positive), and accelerated to **+1.12 on Aug 19** — a 2.83-point total swing from its −1.71 low. A positive MACD after a 12-month downtrend is a meaningful momentum regime shift.
- **MACD histogram** bottomed at **−0.80 on June 25** (coinciding exactly with the RSI capitulation low — a clean momentum trough), crossed positive ~July 17, and has expanded to **+0.80** — now *exceeding* its May 21 level of 0.24.
- **Nuance:** No bearish divergence has appeared during the rally — histogram bars are still expanding, which argues against immediate exhaustion. However, momentum indicators of this shape after a parabolic drop frequently produce *one more* leg up followed by a retest of the $74–75 support before the trend resolves.

## 6. Relative Strength (rsi) — CAPITULATION → HEALTHY RECOVERY

- RSI printed a textbook **capitulation low of 19.05 on June 25** — deeply oversold, consistent with the $70.90 price low and the June 25 volume spike.
- It has since climbed **+44.8 points** to **63.89 (Aug 19)**, decisively above the 50-line.
- **Key nuance:** RSI is bullish but **not overbought** (<70), leaving ~6 points of room before exhaustion. The rally is "healthy continuation" territory. What to monitor: if price makes a *new high* toward $80–85 but RSI fails to exceed ~64, that's a bearish divergence and an early exit warning.

## 7. Volatility (atr) — NORMALIZING; CALIBRATES RISK

- ATR spiked to **2.829 on July 17** (the capitulation session, ~4.1% of price — extreme dispersion), then contracted to **~2.50 by Aug 19** (~3.2% of price).
- **Interpretation:** The market is digesting the shock; consolidation-phase volatility. Practical application:
  - **Stop-loss:** A 1.5–2× ATR stop from any entry (~$3.75–5.00 below entry). For a long entered near $77, that places a stop at **$72–74** — which conveniently aligns with the 50-SMA ($74.59) support confluence.
  - **Position sizing:** Reduce size by ~20–30% relative to normal-vol names; ATR ~2.50 means a 3%+ adverse daily move is routine, so leverage must be kept low.

## 8. Volume Confirmation (vwma) — MIXED: BOUNCE IS VOLUME-BACKED, BUT MEDIUM-TERM WEAK

- VWMA fell from **88.21 (May) → 70.22 (Aug 5 low) → 75.36 (Aug 19)**.
- The recovery from the Aug 5 VWMA trough to 75.36 shows **the bounce has genuine volume participation** — this is not a low-volume technical rebound, which elevates its quality.
- However, VWMA remains 14.5% below its May peak and *below* the 50-SMA's path of prior distribution zones, indicating that **net institutional flow has only just stabilized**, not reversed.

## 4. Actionable Trade Plan

**For existing holders — HOLD, with profit-taking rules:**
- Do not add until price **closes above $80** (psychological + 10-EMA resistance).
- Scale out on rallies into **$82–85** (prior consolidation) and decisively into **$88–89** (200-SMA), *especially* if RSI diverges there.

- **Stop:** Below **$72** (1.5× ATR under entry zone and below the 50-SMA buffer). Hard invalidation: daily close below **$70.5** (July 27 structure) or **$68.90** (the low).

- **Invalidate & reverse bias:** A high-volume close below $68.95 opens the path to **$65 → $60** — trend-followers should treat that as a fresh short/exit signal.

**For long-term trend investors — SELL/avoid:** The 200-SMA is falling and price is far beneath it. No long-term entry until (a) a weekly close above ~$89, AND (b) the 50-SMA has flattened or turned up (likely a mid/late-Q4 2026 decision point). The golden-cross repair is still many months away.

**Key levels summary:**
- Resistance: $78 → $80 → $82–85 → **$88–89 (200-SMA, critical)**
