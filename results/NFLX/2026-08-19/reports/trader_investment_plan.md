NFLX still has durable cash generation, modest net debt, attractive forward earnings versus its historical multiple, and optionality from advertising and live programming. The bear case is not weak: NFLX remains below a falling 200-day, the 50-day is still declining, and Q3 guidance remains a live risk. Because of that, the decision should be conditional on support holding and on execution not breaking.

If average entry is near 75, a stop below 68.95 implies a roughly 9%–10% adverse move, so the total position should generally stay around 8%–10% of the portfolio, adjusted for account size and existing technology exposure.

The key monitoring triggers are Q3 guidance, ad revenue and live programming commentary, content cost trend, subscriber additions, and whether the 50-day stops declining. A macro or AI-rotation sell-off that leaves NFLX holding the **72–74** shelf with intact fundamentals should be treated as an opportunity, not an exit. A weak Q3 guide should be treated as an immediate risk-reduction trigger even if price is still above 72.

Bottom line: the asymmetric risk-reward is improved enough to justify a staged buy in NFLX, but the position must be controlled by support, size, and Q3 proof.
