**As of: 2026-08-18 | Price (Aug 17 close): $343.78**

## 1. Market Context & Price Structure

GOOGL has undergone a complete volatility cycle since March 2026:

- **Mar 2 → Mar 30:** Opened near $305.92 and fell to a cyclical low of **$273.16** (Mar 30) — a ~10% flush that likely washed out weak hands.
- **Mar 30 → May 13:** A powerful impulse rally of **~47%**, peaking at **$402.12** — accompanied by RSI hitting 84 (extreme overbought) and MACD peaking at +22.16.
- **May 13 → present:** A measured correction of **~14.5%** from the highs, currently stabilizing at $343.78. The stock has retraced roughly 54% of the Mar–May gain — a "healthy" 50%+ retracement, not a trend breakdown.
- **Volume:** Activity spiked to 114.7M shares on Jun 26 (capitulation/rotation phase), vs. a 20–40M normal baseline — heavy participation on the downside suggests meaningful hands changed positions.
- Small dividends ($0.21 Mar 9, $0.22 Jun 8) are negligible for technicals.

## 2. Trend Analysis (The Core Conflict)

The central feature of GOOGL right now is a **divergence between timeframes**:

- **Long-term (200 SMA):** Steadily rising from $272.69 (Apr 20) to **$331.63** — an uninterrupted +21.6% climb. Price at $343.78 sits **~3.7% above** this line. The long-term uptrend is firmly intact; the 200 SMA is the key institutional support shelf.
- **Medium-term (50 SMA):** Peaked at $372.44 (Jul 13), now rolling over to **$352.62**. Price is **~2.7% below** it. This is a classic correction-within-an-uptrend signature: price below the falling 50 SMA but above the rising 200 SMA.
- **Net read:** The stock is in **consolidation/correction**, not a bear regime. A reclaim of $352.62–355 (50 SMA + VWMA confluence) would confirm the uptrend resumes; a break below $331.63 (200 SMA) would signal genuine regime deterioration.

## 3. Momentum (Mixed-to-Soft, Improving From Below)

- **MACD line:** Crossed below zero ~Jun 9, hit a trough of **-8.81 on Jul 27**, and has since repaired to **-1.77**. It is approaching the zero line — the final hurdle for bullish confirmation — but has not crossed it.
- **MACD histogram:** Told a more nuanced story: bottomed at -6.18 (Jun 3), recovered to **+4.44 on Aug 4** (a brief bullish resurgence), then **rolled back to -0.56 on Aug 18**. This stall in the recovery is the most important short-term warning — the mid-August bounce has lost steam.
- **RSI:** Completed a full cycle: 84.03 (May 8, overbought) → 31.03 (Jul 23, near-oversold) → 62.57 (Aug 4) → **46.16 (neutral)**. Price has bottomed on an RSI washout and reset to mid-range. Watch the **50.0 pivot**: sustained RSI >50 aligns with momentum recovery; <40 re-opens downside risk.

**Momentum verdict:** The severe selling pressure is over (Jul 23 low was the exhaustion point), but fresh buying has not yet asserted dominance. We are in a momentum vacuum.

## 4. Volatility (Contracting, Still Elevated)

- **ATR:** Ranged from $7.74 (late Apr) to a peak of **$13.92 (Aug 5)**, now **$10.17**. Volatility is cooling from its August spike but remains well above the spring baseline — expect continued wide intraday swings.
- **Bollinger Lower Band:** Rose from $263.67 to $378.34 (May 28) during the blow-off, then contracted to **$313.99**. Band contraction typically precedes a directional break — this setup has the potential for an expansion move once momentum commits.
- **Risk management:** With ATR ~$10 (≈2.9% of price), tight stops will get hunted. **Use ≥2× ATR (~$20) stops** or structure stops below structural levels rather than at arbitrary pips.

## 5. Volume Confirmation (Constructive Sign)

- **VWMA:** Peaked at $392.51 (May 22) with the rally, fell to **$339.95 (Jul 30)** — buyers absorbed the July selling right around the $340 level — and has since firmed to **$354.97**.
- Price ($343.78) sits *just below* the VWMA ($354.97), meaning volume-confirmed average participants are slightly overhead, but the VWMA's recovery from $339.95 shows accumulation at lower levels. The July 30 VWMA trough ($339.95) ≈ price support ($340) is a double-confluence defense zone.
