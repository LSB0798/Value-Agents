

# AAPL Technical Analysis Report

**Instrument:** AAPL (Apple Inc.) | **As of:** 2026-07-07 | **Last Close:** $312.39

## 1. Executive Summary

AAPL has delivered a textbook mature bull trend over the trailing 12 months, rising from a $200.53 floor (Aug 1, 2025) to an all-time high of $314.73 (May 29, 2026) — a ~57% advance — before consolidating near the highs. Price closed at **$312.39** on July 6, 2026, just **0.7% below** the all-time high. The current setup is one of a **strong, well-confirmed uptrend undergoing a short-term V-shaped recovery** after a sharp late-June correction. The bias is **constructive/trend-following long**, but the trade is extended, sits directly under ATH resistance, and is running in an elevated-volatility regime — so it demands disciplined risk management rather than aggressive size.

**Verdict: BUY (tactical, pullback- and level-conditioned) with strict ATR-based risk controls.**

- **Nuance/risk:** Price is **extended** — ~6% above the 50 SMA and ~15% above the 200 SMA. Healthy uptrends mean-revert toward the 50 SMA periodically; chasing into a +15% deviation from the long-term average increases the risk of buying into a pullback.

**Implication:** The 50 SMA at **$294.75** is the line in the sand. The 200 SMA at **$270.83** is the major trend-defining level below.

## 3. Momentum (MACD + RSI)

This is where the current inflection sits — a fresh bullish trigger is forming:

- **MACD line: $1.95** — recovered from a **-$2.90 trough on June 29** (down from a +10.48 peak on May 28). The line has **just re-crossed back into positive territory**, a fresh bullish momentum signal after the late-June bearish phase.
- **MACD Histogram: +2.01** — swung from a **-$3.50 trough (Jun 12)** back to near its mid-May high (+2.07). The V-shaped recovery indicates the bearish pressure has been **absorbed by buyers**, and momentum is re-accelerating. Confirm sustainability above ~+2 to lock in the reversal.
- **RSI: 60.67** — this tells the full short-term story: it was **extreme at 80.03 (May 28, deeply overbought)**, then sold off to **32.22 (Jun 25, oversold)**, and has now recovered to a neutral **60.67**. 

**Interpretation:** The late-June correction was an overextended, overbought-liquidation that reached oversold levels and has since mean-reverted. Crucially, **RSI has recovered from oversold without the price making a new low** — a healthy recovery. RSI at ~60 is mid-range with **headroom before the 70 overbought threshold**, meaning there is still momentum capacity to the upside before the stock becomes statistically stretched again. The alignment of a **fresh positive MACD cross + rising histogram + RSI climbing through neutral** is a constructive confluence.

## 4. Volatility & Risk (ATR)

- **ATR: $8.38** — up sharply from **$6.69 (May 8)**, peaking at **$8.73 (Jul 2)**. Volatility has structurally expanded from a ~$5.5–6.7 regime to a **~$7.0–8.7 regime**.

**Implication for execution:**
- Position swings of $8–8.7/day are now normal — **sizing must be reduced and stops widened** versus the tighter May environment.
- A standard 1.5× ATR stop ≈ **$12.5–13** wide. A tighter 2× ATR ≈ $17. Given price is near the 50 SMA, a structural stop **below $294.75 (the 50 SMA)** — roughly 1.2× ATR below current price — aligns both technical structure and ATR-based risk.
- The elevated ATR also widens expected false breakouts, so avoid over-trading the top of the band and require confirmation (e.g., a close beyond a level) rather than intraday pokes.

- The VWMA sits **below price ($312.39)** but **above the 50 SMA ($294.75)**. Its recovery confirms the late-June profit-taking has been met with **volume-backed re-accumulation**.
- **Nuance:** The VWMA (~$291) is notably **below** both price and the 10 EMA, meaning price is moving **ahead of its volume-weighted average**. This is constructive (smart money is re-engaging) but also a caution flag — a volume-lagging price run can stall if new buying doesn't confirm the next leg. **Watch volume on the attempt to break ATH at $314.73.**

## 6. Actionable Trade Plan

**Bias:** Long / Trend-following (Buy on pullbacks or confirmed breakout).

- **Entry 1 (pullback/mean-reversion):** Accumulate on a dip toward the **50 SMA / VWMA confluence zone ~$291–295**. This is the highest-probability entry with the best risk/reward, buying the deviation back to the dynamic support.
- **Entry 2 (breakout/momentum):** A **confirmed close above the $314.73 ATH on expanding volume** opens a fresh, air-pocket leg higher. This is the aggressive, momentum-based entry.
- **Stop-loss (risk control):**
  - Structural: below the **50 SMA at $294.75** (invalidates medium-term structure).
  - ATR-based: ~1.5× ATR (~$12.5) below entry.
  - Use the level that best fits your timeframe; the 50 SMA break is the trend-termination signal.
- **Position sizing:** Reduce size to match the elevated $8.38 ATR (risk ~1–2% of capital per trade). Do not max out because the trend "looks strong."
- **Targets:**
  - First: measured continuation / round numbers above ATH.
  - Use the rising 50 SMA (~$295) as a **trailing reference** — hold while price keeps making higher closes above it.
- **Invalidation / exit triggers:**
  - RSI re-enters **>75–80** (overbought exhaustion, as in May) → trim into strength.
  - A close **below $294.75 (50 SMA)** → stand aside / reduce; the bullish stack is breaking.
  - A close **below $270.83 (200 SMA)** → trend is effectively over; exit fully.
- **What to monitor weekly:** RSI sustaining **>60** (confirms the reversal is durable), MACD histogram holding **>0 and rising**, VWMA catching up to price (volume confirmation), and ATR regime (if ATR contracts below ~$7, pullback entries get tighter and more reliable).
