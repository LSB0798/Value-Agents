### 3.2 "Taking the Gloves Off" With Nemotron — Up the Stack — *Bullish (strategic), Watch execution*
- **Item 19 (2026-07-06):** VC **Jason Calacanis** argues NVDA is "moving beyond its role as the world's leading AI chipmaker" and "positioning itself to compete more directly with OpenAI and Anthropic" through its **open-weight AI (Nemotron)** portfolio; predicts Jensen Huang will "own the whole AI stack."
- **Read-through:** NVDA is **broadening its moat from silicon → software/models → stack ownership**. Open-weight strategy is a differentiation play (ecosystem lock-in, developer capture) and a direct shot at the labs that drive its GPU demand.
- **Risk/Watch:** Vertical integration can blur the customer/competitor line (labs building on your hardware that are also your customers). Execution and model competitiveness determine whether this is "moat" or "distraction."
