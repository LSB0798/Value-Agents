## 2. Company Overview & Thesis
Amazon has entered what appears to be a **heavy, debt-funded infrastructure/AI capex supercycle** while maintaining double-digit revenue growth. Key structural story over the trailing twelve months:
- **Revenue:** $155.7B (Q1 2025) → $213.4B (Q4 2025 peak) → $181.5B (Q1 2026). Q1 2026 revenue grew **~16.6% YoY**, indicating an accelerated growth trajectory heading into the peak Q4 season.

**Bull case (primary):**
- Revenue growth re-accelerating to ~16.6% YoY with a strong Q4 season ahead; gross margin ~52%.
- Massive but **well-funded** infrastructure/AI buildout ($250B+ PPE growth) with a fortress-like balance sheet (net debt $17.3B, $143B liquidity) — Amazon can outspend rivals through the cycle.
- Net income +77% YoY; even ex one-time gains, core earnings grew.

- **FCF is negative (-$18.2B)** and rising D&A ($18.9B/qtr) will drag future GAAP margins; any deceleration in AWS/AI revenue growth would punish a stock priced for the capex to pay off.
- Debt grew +58% YoY — manageable today, but the trajectory matters if OCF softens further.
- **Watch the Q4 2026 print (Nov 2026):** gross margin sustainability vs. the $103.4B Q4-2025 peak and capex normalization.

**Positioning ideas:**
- **Momentum/growth traders:** Accumate into OCF-weakness (negative FCF headlines) given the balance sheet can absorb it; the negative-FCF print is a funding event, not a failure signal.
- **Value/quality screens:** Expect AMZN to fail FCF and P/E screens mechanically; the reported EPS multiple is artificially low only because of a one-time gain — normalize it before comparing to peers.
- **Hedging:** Rising D&A and debt imply lower reported operating leverage ahead; earnings disappointments from core operations (not gains) will drive the stock.
